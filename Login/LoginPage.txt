## Security Reminder

For your security, never share your password with anyone. Always make sure you are signing in through the official website before entering your account information.

If you are using a shared or public computer, remember to sign out when you are finished.

## Having Trouble?

If you cannot access your account:

* Check that your email address is correct
* Make sure Caps Lock is not enabled
* Try resetting your password
* Check your internet connection
* Contact our support team if the problem continues
